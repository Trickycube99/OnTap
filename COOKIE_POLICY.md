## 1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and web storage technologies (such as HTML5 `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`) are small data files placed on your browser or device when you visit an online platform. They enable our web application to recognize your session, maintain table ordering continuity, remember dietary preferences, and ensure cryptographic platform security.

This Cookie Policy complies with:
- The **EU ePrivacy Directive** (Directive 2002/58/EC as amended)
- **GDPR Article 6** (Lawful consent requirements)
- **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)**

---

## 2. Our Privacy-First Approach: Zero Third-Party Trackers

- **Zero Third-Party Advertising Cookies:** We do **NOT** use third-party marketing cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling pixels.
- **Self-Hosted Web Fonts:** We do not load fonts dynamically from external Google CDNs. All typography files are self-hosted locally, preventing visitor IP address transmission without consent.
- **Session Replay Prohibited:** We strictly do not inject keystroke recorders or session replay tools (e.g., FullStory, LogRocket, Hotjar).

## 3. Categories of Web Storage & Cookies We Use

### A. Strictly Necessary (Always Active)
These technologies are technically essential to deliver the core service and cannot be disabled in our consent settings.

| Key / Cookie Name | Storage Type | Purpose | Lifespan |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ontap_session_role` | HttpOnly Cookie | Secures authenticated staff & admin sessions | 24 Hours |
| `flowscape_auth_sessions_v3` | `sessionStorage` | Stores active role credentials securely | Browser Tab Session |
| `flowscape_guest_scanned_qr` | `sessionStorage` | Validates cryptographically signed table QR scan | Browser Tab Session |
| `flowscape_guest_table` | `sessionStorage` | Retains table number to deliver food to your seat | Browser Tab Session |
| `ontap_cookie_consent_v1` | `localStorage` | Records your privacy & cookie preferences | 12 Months |

### B. Functional & Preference Storage (Optional)
Enables helpful customization and personalized app settings.

| Key / Cookie Name | Storage Type | Purpose | Default State |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ontap_lang` | `localStorage` | Remembers chosen interface language (English / Hindi) | Opt-In (Active upon selection) |
| `ontap_theme_mode` | `localStorage` | Remembers UI visual mode (Light / Dark) | Opt-In (Active upon selection) |
| `ontap_diner_name` | `sessionStorage` | Remembers optional table guest name | Opt-In (User provided) |

### C. Analytics & Performance (Strictly Blocked by Default)
Helps measure aggregated, anonymous platform performance and error metrics.

| Identifier | Purpose | Default State |
|---|---|---|
| Performance Telemetry | Anonymous load-time diagnostics & crash reporting | **Strictly Blocked until explicit affirmative consent** |

## 4. How to Manage & Revoke Your Cookie Consent

1. **In-App Cookie Banner & Preferences:** When you first visit OnTap, our compliant Cookie Consent Banner prompts you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. Non-essential categories remain disabled by default.
2. **Re-Opening Cookie Preferences:** You may change your preferences or withdraw consent at any time by clicking the **"Cookie Preferences"** link in our website footer.
3. **Browser Controls:** Most web browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through browser settings (e.g. Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that blocking Strictly Necessary storage may prevent table ordering and staff logins from functioning.
