## 1. Overview & Consumer Protection Commitment

OnTap is committed to transparent, fair, and consumer-friendly commercial transactions in compliance with the **Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Act**, the **EU Consumer Rights Directive (Directive 2011/83/EU)**, and applicable regional consumer protection legislation.

This policy delineates the specific terms governing:
1. **Commercial Software Subscriptions** purchased by restaurant owners/operators; and
2. **Food & Dining Orders** placed by diners via table QR codes or counter POS terminals.

---

## 2. Commercial Software Subscriptions (Restaurant Owners)

### A. 14-Day Statutory Cooling-Off & Satisfaction Guarantee
- If you subscribe to any paid recurring tier (e.g. Starter, Pro, Enterprise), you may cancel your subscription within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of initial activation for a full 100% refund of your initial subscription fee—no questions asked.
- To request your initial refund, simply email `billing@ontap.app` or trigger 1-click cancellation within your Admin dashboard.

### B. Recurring Monthly & Annual Renewals
- **Advance Renewal Reminders:** In accordance with the FTC Negative Option Rule, subscribers receive conspicuous email reminders prior to annual or major recurring renewals.
- **Mid-Cycle Cancellations:** You may cancel recurring auto-renewal at any time without fees or penalties. Cancellation halts future charges immediately. Your subscription remains active through the end of the current paid billing period.
- **Service Outage Credits:** In the rare event of verified platform unavailability exceeding our 99.5% uptime service level agreement (SLA), merchants are entitled to pro-rated billing credits upon submission of a support ticket.

## 3. Food, Beverage & Dining Orders (Diners & Guests)

### A. Table Orders Prior to Kitchen Preparation
- Because restaurant meals are perishable and prepared custom-to-order, a diner may cancel an unfulfilled ticket only **before** the kitchen begins physical cooking or item preparation.
- Once an order ticket transitions to `"Cooking"` or `"Served"`, kitchen ingredients have been committed, and statutory food safety rules prevent restocking or standard cancellation.

### B. Defective, Incorrect, or Quality Issues
If an item delivered to your table:
- Is materially different from what was ordered;
- Contains undisclosed allergens that were clearly noted in order customization notes;
- Suffers from temperature, spoilage, or hygiene defects;

**Remedy:** You are entitled to an immediate replacement at no cost or a full immediate refund issued by the restaurant counter staff. If paid via digital UPI QR, the restaurant manager can initiate an instant digital refund to your source account.

### C. Escrow & Platform Dispute Escalation
If you encounter an unresolved billing dispute with a merchant regarding an order placed through OnTap, contact our mediation team at `disputes@ontap.app`. We investigate all disputes within 48 business hours to ensure consumer fairness.

## 4. How to Request a Refund

1. **For Subscriptions:**
   - Email: `billing@ontap.app` or `refunds@ontap.app` (Subject: *Subscription Refund Request*)
   - Provide your registered business email and store name.
2. **For Diner Orders:**
   - Request assistance directly at the restaurant cashier counter; or
   - Submit a ticket with receipt screenshot to `disputes@ontap.app`.

---

## 5. Refund Processing Timelines

- Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original method of payment (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, or Net Banking).
- Refunds typically appear on your billing statement within **3 to 7 business days**, depending on your financial institution's settlement speed.
